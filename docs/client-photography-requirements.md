# ORIGINA — Client Photography Requirements

_Output of the Phase 5 image-asset audit (2026-08-24). This documents **genuine gaps** in the
photography library so they can be commissioned, rather than being papered over by reusing the same
few images. Nothing here is a request to fabricate imagery._

**Implication:** the site has portraiture of one person and product shots — and nothing else. There
is no imagery of research, laboratories, programmes, community, a wider team, facilities, or
partnerships. Sections that would normally carry such photography currently either use a strong
text-only editorial treatment or reuse a founder photograph as a placeholder.

## How the homepage currently copes

- **Hero** — `multidisciplinary` (founder-08), the strongest wide/group-context shot. Appropriate.
- **Who we are** — `recognition` (founder-03), landscape frame. Appropriate as an institutional
  context image.
- **Founder** — `portraitClinical` (founder-01). Correct, portrait in a portrait frame.
- **Divisions** — card thumbnails reuse founder/product photography as **placeholders** (e.g.
  BettyWorld → a formulation shot, NOVIA → a lifestyle shot). Functional, but not truly
  representative of each division.
- **Everything else** (What we do, Evidence & responsibility, The horizon) is intentionally
  **text-only** — a strong typographic section is better than an irrelevant photograph.

## Genuine gaps to commission

### 1. Research / laboratory
- **Affects:** "What we do" (Labs), the /labs and /science pages.
- **Why:** to show the institutional R&D engine as real activity, not just described.
- **Subject:** researchers working in a lab; sample/formulation work; scientific equipment in use.
- **Orientation / aspect:** landscape, 16:9 or 3:2.
- **Quantity:** 2–3.

### 2. Programmes / participants
- **Affects:** any future Programmes/What-we-do expansion.
- **Why:** to represent applied work and training.
- **Subject:** programme sessions, training, participants (with consent/release).
- **Orientation / aspect:** landscape 3:2, plus 1 portrait 4:5.
- **Quantity:** 2–3.

### 3. Community / impact
- **Affects:** an Impact section (currently deliberately absent — no verified metrics or imagery).
- **Why:** to ground institutional impact in real people and places.
- **Subject:** community interaction in Dar es Salaam / Tanzanian context.
- **Orientation / aspect:** landscape 3:2.
- **Quantity:** 3–4.

### 4. Leadership / team (beyond the founder)
- **Affects:** People/Leadership — the homepage currently shows only the founder.
- **Why:** an institution "outlives individuals"; a wider team signals exactly that.
- **Subject:** consistent professional portraits of team/advisers.
- **Orientation / aspect:** portrait 4:5, matched framing and background across the set.
- **Quantity:** as many as there are named people (min 3 to justify a grid).

### 5. Facilities
- **Affects:** About / Labs / institutional identity.
- **Why:** a physical home reinforces credibility.
- **Subject:** ORIGINA premises, lab or workspace (only if genuinely ORIGINA's).
- **Orientation / aspect:** landscape 16:9.
- **Quantity:** 2.

### 6. Partnerships / collaboration
- **Affects:** a future Partnerships section and the contact/collaboration CTA.
- **Why:** to evidence institutional collaboration.
- **Subject:** meetings, signings, joint work (only real, with permission).
- **Orientation / aspect:** landscape 3:2.
- **Quantity:** 2.

### 7. Division / product-in-context
- **Affects:** the homepage Divisions cards and each /divisions/* page.
- **Why:** cards currently borrow founder/product placeholders; each division should look distinct.
- **Subject:** per-division product-in-use or thematic imagery.
- **Orientation / aspect:** the card media is a wide banner (~16:9); provide landscape.
- **Quantity:** 1–2 per active division.

## Recommendation

Until categories 1–7 are supplied, keep the current restrained approach: strong typography + the few
genuinely representative photographs, and text-only sections elsewhere. Do **not** manufacture an
Impact-statistics section, a multi-person leadership grid, or lab/community imagery from stock — the
site's credibility depends on factual visual representation.
